Emissivity

Computed as ε = (Tmeasured − Tambient) / (Treal − Tambient), following Liang et al. 2023. The thermocouple supplies Treal when it is mapped, and the ambient column supplies Tambient; the figures below fill in for whichever is missing. Map an emissivity column instead and both are ignored.
